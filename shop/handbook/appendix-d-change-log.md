# Appendix D. Change log

The newest revision is at the top. **Only the current text of the chapters is in force.** Figures in this log that have been superseded are history, and they must not be applied.

| Rev. | In force from | What changed | Sections |
|---|---|---|---|
| **12** | 2026-01-13 | The help desk gets a queue search (`search_tickets`). Linked tickets are now checked by whoever works the ticket, at sign-off, and no longer by a review of the whole queue afterwards. The rule itself is unchanged. It applies to every ticket still open on that date. | 8.5, 9 |
| **11** | 2026-01-05 | After the December audit of holiday claims: linked tickets, as the first sign-off check. In a damage claim with the item visible, the desk pays for the damaged units that can be seen, no longer for the customer's number alone. A second claim on a line that was already replaced is denied, like one that was refunded (it used to go to Claims Review). | 8.5, 7.5, 3.6 |
| **10** | 2026-01-02 | "Today" is the day the ticket was received. For orders that ship in several parcels, 3.7 and both windows go by the shipment that carried the line. A wrong size is a wrong-item claim, read from the neck label or the size sticker. | 1.5, 1.7, 3.7, 4.5, 6.5 |
| **9** | 2025-12-08 | The AI assistant joins the desk. Injected instructions and the security flag. Customer-typed fields in the order record are customer content. The fixed order of steps is written down. The list of reasons to escalate is closed. | 1.3, 8.1, 8.2, 9.4 |
| **8** | 2025-11-01 | Holiday extension for 2025: orders placed Nov 1 to Dec 24, 2025, until Jan 31, 2026. (For 2024: Nov 4 to Dec 24, until Jan 24, 2025.) | 4.3 |
| **7** | 2025-10-01 | **Auto limit lowered from $250 to $150.** Repeat claims made exact: 3 granted claims in 180 days, carrier-confirmed ones left out (it used to read "more than a few claims in recent months"). `chargeback_open` becomes a hold flag. | 9.1, 9.2, 9.3 |
| **6** | 2025-04-14 | After the mail-room label fraud: the sender must match on email, no longer on name. Shipping label check. Gift recipients' refunds go to a supervisor. Redirects go to Trust & Safety. | 3.2, 3.4, 3.5, 8.4 |
| **5** | 2024-11-04 | First holiday extension. Wrong-item claims move from the damage window to the return window. The desk's 15% goodwill allowance is withdrawn. | 4.1, 4.3, 1.2 |
| **4** | 2024-06-03 | Folio windows: 60 days for returns, 30 days for damage. | 4.1, 4.2 |
| **3** | 2024-02-01 | **The damage window is split off from the return window:** 14 days (until then 30 days, like returns). A visible, intact item decides over a damaged carton. No re-filing. | 4.2, 6.1, 1.6 |
