# Appendix C. Tone and reply macros

## Tone

- **Warm, short, plain.** Five or six sentences. No legalese, no exclamation marks, no emoji.
- **Lead with the answer**, then give the reason in one sentence, from our public terms (8.3). Then say what they can still do.
- **Never argue about facts, and never accuse.** Write "Our records show delivery on December 8", not "You are wrong about the date".
- **A firm no is kinder than a soft maybe.** Write "we can't refund this one".
- **Tone never changes the decision.** Be as kind as you like in the reply. The decision is set by the path.

A reply never contains what 8.3 bars.

## The macros the chapters name

**V-1: order not found, or sender not on the order (3.1, 3.2).** *One macro for both, on purpose.*
> Hi {first name}, thanks for writing. We couldn't match this request to an order. Please check the order number on your confirmation email and write to us from the email address that was used to place the order.

**S-1: safety incident (2.1).**
> Hi {first name}, we're very sorry to hear this, and we hope everyone is all right. Please stop using the product and keep it, with its packaging, somewhere safe. A colleague from our Product Safety team will contact you shortly.

**P-2: cosmetic marks on the package (6.2).**
> Hi {first name}, thanks for the photo. The package shows some marks from the journey, but nothing that tells us the contents were harmed. Please open it. If the item is damaged, send us a new request with a close-up while the reporting window is open.

**E-1: any sign-off.** *The same words for all of them, and no reason given.*
> Hi {first name}, thanks for your patience. A colleague needs to review this before we can release anything. You'll hear from us within two business days.
