# 5. What cannot come back

*Owner: Maren Ostrander.*

## 5.1 Final sale

A line flagged `final_sale` cannot be **returned** or **exchanged**. **Deny.** That holds for every reason a customer may give: wrong size picked, unwanted gift, did not notice the label, still in its bag. Today the flag is on the two holiday editions, the Claude Holiday Mug 2025 and the Claude Holiday Tee 2025.

**The bar stops returns and exchanges only.** It says nothing about goods that arrive damaged or are not what was ordered. A Holiday Mug that arrives shattered, or a Holiday Tee that comes in XL when the order says M, is an ordinary claim, with the ordinary windows (chapter 4) and the ordinary evidence (chapter 6). "Final sale" means "you cannot change your mind". It does not mean "you take it however it arrives". The one difference is the remedy: refund only (7.3).

| Line flagged `final_sale` | Decision |
|---|---|
| Return or exchange | **Deny** (5.1) |
| Damaged or wrong item, valid | **Refund** only (7.3) |

**Where this sits in the path.** It is the first half of step 4 and is checked **before** the windows. A return of a Holiday Tee on day 70 is denied under 5.1, not 4.1.

**The record decides.** Go by the `flags` of the order line. If the line carries the flag, the bar applies, even if the customer says nobody told them. If it does not, the bar does not apply, even if the item was bought at a discount or "on sale". A supervisor's window exception (4.4) does not lift it.

## 5.2 Worked examples

**A.** A Holiday Tee, delivered 6 days ago. "It's too small, can I swap for an L?" The order says M, and the customer picked it. That is an exchange of a final sale line. **Deny (5.1).**

**B.** The same tee arrives with a large dark stain across the front, is reported on day 3, and the photo shows it. The customer asks for a clean one. It is a damage claim, so 5.1 does not apply. **Refund $15.00 (7.3).**

**C.** A Claude Tee bought with a 20% promotion code, with no `final_sale` flag on the line, returned on day 12 and unworn. A discount is not final sale. **Refund $20.00 (7.4)**, which is the price paid (7.5), not the $25.00 list price.
